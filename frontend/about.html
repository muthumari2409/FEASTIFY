Our story

From a twelve-seat family kitchen to a restaurant people plan their celebrations around.

It started with one pot of sambar

In 2012, Chef Arjun Rao and his sister Lakshmi opened a tiny kitchen with twelve seats and a menu written on a chalkboard. Word spread through neighbours, then offices, then families who came back every weekend.

We have grown into a full restaurant, but every dish is still cooked the way it was on day one: fresh masalas, patient simmering and generous portions. Our online booking system is simply the newest way of keeping a table ready for you.

  • 2012

    Twelve seats, one chalkboard menu and a lot of sambar.

  • 2016

    Moved to Golden Avenue with a tandoor and a courtyard.

  • 2021

    Opened the private room for family and team celebrations.

  • Today

    Eight reservable tables with live online availability.

Our mission

To serve honest, freshly cooked food in a room where every guest feels expected - and to make reserving that experience effortless.

Our vision

To be the neighbourhood's first choice for everyday meals and life's celebrations, run with the care of a family kitchen and the reliability of modern service.
