A kitchen built around the slow simmer

Feastify began as a twelve-seat family kitchen. Today we cook for hundreds each week, but the rule has not changed: spices are roasted in-house every morning, gravies simmer for hours, and bread goes into the tandoor only when you order.

Come for a quick lunch or a long celebration dinner. Eight tables, from cosy two-seaters to a private room for eight, can be reserved online with live availability.
